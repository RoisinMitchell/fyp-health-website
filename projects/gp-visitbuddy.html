GP VisitBuddy An Edge AI Solution for Patient Recall

Sarah Kelly

GP VisitBuddy is a privacy-first mobile app that improves patient recall of medical instructions by recording, transcribing, and structuring GP consultations entirely on-device. It records and transcribes consultations entirely on the user�s device, then extracts key medical instructions such as medications, follow-ups, and safety advice. By turning complex conversations into clear, structured summaries, the app improves patient understanding, recall, and confidence without relying on cloud processing.

Project Snapshot

Helping patients leave appointments with clarity

Patients can forget up to 40-80% of the information provided during medical consultations, particularly details about medications and follow-up care. This may be due to receiving large amounts of information or processing difficult news. This can lead to confusion, poor adherence to treatment, and increased anxiety. GP VisitBuddy addresses this problem by providing a patient-controlled app that captures consultations and presents them as clear, structured summaries.

The app uses on-device speech recognition (Whisper.cpp) to transcribe consultations with no cloud processing, ensuring full data privacy and GDPR-compliant local processing. Before recording, users confirm GP consent through an in-app prompt to support transparency and trust.

A schema-guided system then extracts key information such as medications, follow-ups, tests, and safety advice. The app follows a human-in-the-loop AI approach, allowing the GP to review and edit the extracted information before it is finalised, ensuring clinical accuracy while maintaining transparency and trust.

Once confirmed, users can act on the information immediately with options to automatically add medications to a personal list or converting follow-up appointments into calendar events. All data is stored locally on the device in encrypted files.

Unlike cloud-based clinician tools, GP VisitBuddy is designed for patients and carers, with multi-account support, accessibility-focused design, and fully encrypted local storage. The project demonstrates how Edge AI can deliver real-world healthcare benefits while preserving privacy and supporting, rather than replacing, human decision-making.

Focus

Improving patient recall and understanding of medical consultations using fully offline, privacy-preserving Edge AI.

Audience

Patients, carers, and healthcare stakeholders interested in accessible, privacy-first digital health solutions.

Demo

A live demonstration of recording a simulated GP consultation, generating an on-device transcript, and automatically extracting clear, structured medical instructions with no internet connection.
