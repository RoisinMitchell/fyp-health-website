Project Snapshot

Making complex treatment pathways easier to follow

In vitro fertilisation (IVF) is a complex, multi-stage medical process that requires patients to understand a wide range of clinical procedures, timelines, and treatment stages. While fertility clinics provide written information, existing visual material is often sparse and fails to capture the IVF journey as a coherent whole. This can make it difficult for patients to form an overall understanding of the process and what to expect at each stage of treatment. The aim of this project is to explore whether structured, AI-generated visuals can improve the clarity and coherence of IVF patient information.

This project showcases the design of a prompt-engineering framework for generating synthetic IVF journey visuals. The framework adopts a top-down, user-centred approach in which the IVF process is broken up into a series of distinct scenes. A dual-agent architecture is employed to balance visual quality and medical accuracy. These agents are supported by reference-image grounding, multimodal feature extraction, and human-in-the-loop refinement. Generated visuals were evaluated through qualitative comparison with existing IVF clinic infographics, using feedback from study participants to explore the quality of the generated visuals.

Focus

Evaluating whether AI-generated visuals can improve patient understanding and communication of the IVF process compared to existing materials.

Audience

People looking to learn more about what the IVF journey entails.

Demo

Outputs of the agentic framework alongside results from a user study comparing AI-generated visuals with real clinic infographics.
