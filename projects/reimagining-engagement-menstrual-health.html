Reimagining Engagement in Menstrual Health

Project Snapshot

Designing gentler ways to capture cognitive symptoms

Current menstrual tracking applications are widely used but focus primarily on physical symptoms and period prediction. While these tools are helpful, they can feel repetitive and provide limited meaningful insight, which can lead to reduced engagement over time. Many individuals report experiencing cognitive changes such as brain fog, difficulty concentrating, and reduced mental clarity throughout their cycle, yet these experiences are rarely supported within existing tools.

This project investigates how these cognitive experiences can be better represented and understood through interaction design. Using user research, including surveys and interviews, the project identifies key challenges such as difficulty recognizing patterns, lack of insight from current apps, and the influence of contextual factors like sleep and stress.

The proposed design introduces a menstrual health application that integrates cognitive tracking into existing tracking practices. Users can complete quick daily check-ins to record their perceived cognitive state and optionally engage in short, game-like cognitive tasks. These interactions are designed to be lightweight, accessible, and engaging, reducing the burden typically associated with tracking. The application also focuses on meaningful data visualisation, allowing users to explore how their cognitive experiences change across different phases of their cycle.

Rather than aiming for clinical measurement, the project focuses on supporting user awareness and engagement. The goal is to demonstrate how thoughtful, user-centred design can transform health tracking from a repetitive task into a more meaningful and insightful experience.

Focus

Designing an engaging and user-centred way to support awareness of cognitive experiences within menstrual health tracking

Audience

Anyone who experiences a menstrual cycle and wants to better understand how it may relate to their daily focus, productivity, and overall wellbeing.

Demo

An interactive prototype showcasing daily cognitive check-ins, optional game-like tasks, and visualised insights that demonstrate how users can track and explore their cognitive experiences across their cycle.
