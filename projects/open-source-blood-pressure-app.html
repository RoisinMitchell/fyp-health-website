Stage 02 | Detecting Health Issues

Open-source Android Mobile Application for Tracking, Monitoring and Analysing Blood Pressure Readings

Michal Kornacki

An android mobile application for journaling blood pressure readings. Unlike other apps in this space in android, which force you into receiving intrusive ad, forced online account creation, in-app purchases and other unwanted features, this app will be offline first, privacy focused and open source, meaning that your precious medical data is truly yours unlike a cloud-first approach and can be externally audited due to its open-source nature.

Project Snapshot

Making blood pressure trends easier to understand

High blood pressure is a chronic illness affecting an estimated 1.4 billion adults worldwide. Left untreated, it can lead to more serious health risks such as heart attack or stroke. That is why I designed an android mobile app to help with monitoring and logging blood pressure.

While there are many app on android that already do this, they often come with major drawback including intrusive advertising (hindering the user experience), forced cloud and online account creation (leading to user medical data possibly being exposed to third parties) and in-app purchases (making apps less accessible to users on lower incomes). This is why I wanted to create an app that was offline first, add free and privacy respecting that is also open source.

The app is built in the Kotlin programming language using jetpack compose as the UI toolkit, with separate data and UI layers and an MVVM architecture. It uses room and datastore libraries for local data storage. For testing, compose test was used for UI testing and Junit4 for unit testing.

Focus

Open source, offline first, privacy focused, ad free blood pressure journaling android mobile application built with Kotlin, Jetpack compose, MVVM architecture, with separate UI and data layer.

Audience

This app is for anyone who needs to continuously monitor and record their blood pressure and prefers an add free, privacy focused, offline first app where your medical data is your own and not sold off to advertisers or other third parties.
