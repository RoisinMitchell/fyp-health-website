Stage 06 | Recovery & Ongoing Care

Multi-Agent AI System for Safe Addiction Recovery Support

Róisín Mitchell

This project presents a multi-agent AI system designed to provide safe, empathetic support for individuals in addiction recovery. By combining a conversational language model with independent verification and safety agents, the system delivers high-quality dialogue while ensuring that unsafe or harmful content is never shown to the user.

Project Snapshot

Designing AI support with safety in mind

Addiction recovery is a complex and highly sensitive process, where access to timely, non-judgemental support can make a critical difference. While conversational AI offers the potential to provide scalable, 24/7 assistance, existing systems face a fundamental trade-off: they are either safe but rigid, or flexible but unsafe. This project addresses that gap through a novel multi-agent architecture.

The system is built as a sequential pipeline of three specialised AI agents. The first agent generates empathetic, human-like responses tailored to addiction recovery conversations. The second agent verifies the factual grounding of these responses using retrieval-augmented techniques, ensuring that information is accurate and evidence-based. The third and most critical agent enforces safety through a dual-layer moderation system, combining deterministic rules with machine learning-based guardrails to block unsafe content and handle crisis scenarios appropriately.

The system was deployed as a full cloud-native application on AWS and evaluated using a controlled test set. Results show a significant improvement in safety, reducing unsafe outputs by over 4x compared to a baseline model, while maintaining high conversational quality. Importantly, the final system achieved zero safety violations in critical scenarios, demonstrating that safety can be enforced structurally without sacrificing user experience.

This work contributes a practical framework for building safe conversational AI in healthcare-adjacent domains, showing that separating generation, verification, and safety into distinct agents enables both flexibility and reliability.

Focus

Designing a safe, multi-agent conversational AI system that balances empathetic support with strict safety enforcement in addiction recovery.

Audience

Healthcare professionals, AI researchers, and anyone interested in responsible AI systems for mental health and addiction support.

Demo

A live demonstration of the system responding to real-world recovery scenarios, highlighting how unsafe or crisis inputs are intercepted and safely handled in real time while maintaining natural, supportive conversation.
