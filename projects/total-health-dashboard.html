Total Health Dashboard

Sean Mulcahy

Total Health Dashboard is an android based application that provides users with a unified view of their overall health by combining data from multiple sources. It integrates metrics related to physical activity, nutrition, and mental health into a single personalised dashboard, generating a health score out of 100 based on user-defined goals. The aim is to give users a clearer understanding of their overall lifestyle based on the goals they value, rather than focusing on just one aspect of health.

Project Snapshot

Bringing everyday health signals together

Total Health Dashboard is an Android application developed in Android Studio that aims to provide a more complete view of personal health compared to existing apps. Many current health applications focus on only one or two aspects of wellbeing, such as fitness or diet, and often restrict features behind paywalls. This project addresses that gap by bringing together multiple aspects of health including physical activity, nutrition, and mental wellbeing into a single, unified dashboard. Users can define their own goals and receive a personalised health score out of 100, allowing them to track progress in a way that reflects what they personally value.

The application integrates multiple external APIs, including Fitbit for activity data, Open Food Facts for nutritional information, and Zen Quotes to support mental wellbeing features. It follows an MVVM architecture to separate concerns and improve maintainability, with a Room database used for local storage of health-related data and Firebase handling user authentication. The health score is calculated based on user-defined goals, with the flexibility to include or exclude specific metrics depending on user preference. This allows the system to adapt to different lifestyles rather than enforcing a fixed definition of health. Some features, such as broader coverage of social wellbeing, are planned as future improvements as development continues.

Focus

Creating a health app that can be personalised to each user while providing the users a score based on their personalised goals all while including all aspects of health

Audience

Individuals interested in understanding and improving their overall health, from casual users to those actively working towards specific lifestyle goals.

Demo

Show the app and all its feature and how it can be useful, highlighting a gap in the market for health apps, demonstrate the technical skills I used to achieve this.
